Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bindaas BLR ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service at bindaas-blr.vercel.app.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account using Google Sign-In, we receive your name and email address from Google. We use this only to identify your account and associate any content you submit, such as garbage reports and uploaded images, with your profile.

User-Submitted Content

If you submit a garbage report, we collect the location name, GPS coordinates, description, severity, and any photographs you choose to upload. This content is stored in our database and may be visible to other users of the platform.

Usage Data

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with Bindaas BLR. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and device type. This data is collected anonymously and is used solely to improve the platform. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and maintain your session. Google Analytics also sets cookies to track usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of the service may not function correctly without them.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Bindaas BLR platform
  • To display your submitted garbage reports and images on the platform
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve the service using Google Analytics
  • To associate your account with content you submit

Third-Party Services

Bindaas BLR uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics
  • Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) — account authentication
  • Supabase — database and file storage
  • TomTom Traffic — live traffic data
  • OpenWeatherMap — weather data
  • Google Gemini — AI-generated traffic predictions
  • Vercel — hosting and deployment

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Submitted garbage reports and uploaded images are retained to maintain the platform's historical data. You may contact us to request deletion of your account or any content you have submitted.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Bindaas BLR is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
